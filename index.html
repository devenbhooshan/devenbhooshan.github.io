Hi! I'm Deven and I create products and businesses. Recently, I've fallen in love with chess (and I suck at it). My girlfriend puts up with all of it, which makes her easily the best thing in my life.

Now

I'm building Supergrow — helping B2B teams turn employee voices into growth engines. We help companies unlock the power of their employees' personal brands on LinkedIn to drive business growth.

More recently I started WarmDogo, an outbound agent for LinkedIn that finds warm leads in the signals people already give off.

Before

At heart, I'm a software engineer who sees coding as an art form. This perspective was shaped by my experience at GoJek where I learned to appreciate both the technical elegance and real-world impact of well-crafted software.

After working at Amazon, I realized something important — I don't want to be another engineer in a big tech company. I want to build products that I truly care about.
